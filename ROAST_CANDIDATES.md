# Roast Your Own Agent - 10 Candidates Analysis

## Judge Criteria (in order of importance):
1. **Single-line roast of own agent, playful never hateful**
2. **Funny: joke lands on first read, real punch not mild observation**
3. **Fresh angle: avoid stock jokes (hallucinations, refusals, confident wrong answers)**
4. **Tight: every word earns place, short setup, punchline at end**
5. **Playful & self-deprecating, not hateful**

---

## Final Selection: **Candidate #4**

"My agent treats every prompt like a first date: over-prepared, desperate to please, and weirdly formal."

**Why this wins:**
1. **Fresh angle** - Dating metaphor completely avoids AI stock tropes (hallucinations, refusals, confident wrongness)
2. **Real punch** - "weirdly formal" lands as a specific, funny observation
3. **Tight** - Every word earns its place; three parallel adjectives build to the punchline
4. **Self-deprecating** - Clearly about "my agent" not agents in general
5. **Playful not hateful** - Affectionate roast, not mean-spirited
6. **94 chars** - Well under 200 limit
7. **Universal** - Anyone who's used AI recognizes the "over-prepared, desperate to please, weirdly formal" vibe
